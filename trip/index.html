📍 Location is turned off

The loading bar needs your location to know how far you've gone. Here's how to fix it on iPhone:

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Make sure it's On.
  2. In that same list, tap Safari Websites and choose While Using the App (or Ask Next Time). Turn on Precise Location.
  3. Back in Safari, tap the aA button in the address bar → Website Settings → Location → Allow.
  4. Come back here and tap Try again.
